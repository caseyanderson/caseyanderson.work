## a wave
2016 - present

<Cover src="AWP-LOGO-WHITE.svg" />

[a wave](https://www.awave.la/en) is supporting independent, experimental work at the intersection of creative practice (often involving music/sound), education, and community organization. Owned and operated by Casey Anderson, projects by AW are frequently collaborative and feature a range of individuals, organizations, and communities as partners.
